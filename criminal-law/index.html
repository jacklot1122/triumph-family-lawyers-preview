Charges We Defend Every Week

Whatever you have been charged with, you are not the first person to walk into our office with it — and there is almost always more that can be done than people expect.

Drink Driving

A low-range PCA is not automatically a conviction and not automatically a licence loss. A section 10 dismissal or a conditional release order without conviction is realistic in the right case, properly prepared.

What matters is the material you put in front of the magistrate: the traffic offenders programme, character references, and a clear account of what the licence means for your work and family.

  • Low, mid and high range PCA
  • Refuse or fail to submit to a breath test
  • Driving under the influence
  • Section 10 and conditional release order applications
  • Licence appeals and work licence options

Drug Charges

Possession, supply and deemed supply are treated very differently by the courts, and the line between them is often narrower than police allege. Quantity, packaging and text messages all get argued over.

We also look hard at how the drugs were found. An unlawful search is one of the most common reasons a drug charge is thrown out entirely.

  • Possession and self-administration
  • Supply, deemed supply and ongoing supply
  • Cultivation and manufacture
  • Importation and commercial quantities
  • Challenging the legality of a search

Assault

Assault charges cover a very wide range, from common assault to grievous bodily harm with intent. So does the sentencing — some resolve without conviction, others carry standard non-parole periods.

Self-defence is a genuine and frequently successful defence in NSW. So is a careful challenge to whether the injury meets the threshold the charge alleges.

  • Common assault
  • Assault occasioning actual bodily harm
  • Reckless wounding and grievous bodily harm
  • Affray and violent disorder
  • Assault police and resist arrest
  • Self-defence and defence of another

Traffic Offences

Losing your licence is often a bigger practical problem than the fine. For tradespeople, drivers and parents it can cost the job.

Many traffic matters can be dealt with by way of a licence appeal or a section 10, and demerit point suspensions can be appealed to the Local Court.

  • Dangerous and negligent driving
  • Drive whilst disqualified or suspended
  • Police pursuit (Skye's Law)
  • Speeding and demerit point appeals
  • Licence suspension and disqualification appeals
  • Accidents causing injury or death

Apprehended Violence Orders

An AVO is not a criminal conviction, but consenting to one without advice can cost you contact with your children, your firearms licence and in some cases your employment.

We defend AVO applications, negotiate conditions that are workable, and act for people who genuinely need the protection of one.

  • Defending police and private AVO applications
  • Consenting without admissions, where appropriate
  • Varying or revoking existing orders
  • Breach of AVO charges
  • AVOs running alongside a family law matter

Sex Offences

These are the most serious matters we handle and the ones where early, experienced representation matters most. They are also the matters where reputational damage begins long before any hearing.

Everything you tell us is privileged. We will advise you frankly on the strength of the case and we will not pressure you toward a plea you do not accept.

  • Sexual assault and aggravated sexual assault
  • Sexual touching and sexual act offences
  • Child abuse material offences
  • Historical allegations
  • Suppression orders and protecting your identity
  • Police interviews — do not attend without a lawyer
