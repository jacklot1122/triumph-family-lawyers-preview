## What is NOT real here

- **Forms don't send.** There's no PHP behind a static export. On the live site
  they email the firm immediately. Submitting here shows a note saying so.
- **Figures are unconfirmed.** The stats band, professional body badges and
  client reviews were carried over from the criminal law site and still need
  the firm's sign-off. Every one is removable in one click.
- **The logo is the wrong one.** Still the Sydney Triumph Lawyers mark — a
  family law version is needed.
- **Team photos are missing.** The cards show initials until headshots are added.

The page carries a banner saying all of this, and the site is set to noindex so
it can't compete with the real site in search.
