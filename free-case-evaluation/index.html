The Two Things That Decide Your Future

Almost every separation comes down to these. Getting either one wrong is expensive, and in most cases permanent.

Financial & Asset Division

There is no automatic fifty-fifty split. The court identifies the asset pool, weighs what each of you contributed, then adjusts for future needs — a step that commonly moves the outcome by five to fifteen per cent.

Superannuation is part of the pool and can be split by order. It is also the asset people most often sign away without realising. Before you agree to anything, you should know what the pool is actually worth.

  • Full disclosure, valuations and asset tracing
  • Superannuation splitting orders
  • The family home — keep, refinance or sell
  • Businesses, trusts and inheritances
  • Binding financial agreements and consent orders

Parenting & Custody

The law does not start from a presumption of equal time. It starts from one question: what is in the best interests of the child, with their safety weighed above everything else.

That changes what is worth arguing about. A parenting matter is won by presenting a workable, stable arrangement — not by proving your former partner was a poor spouse. We build the arrangement, then make it binding.

  • Live-with and spend-time arrangements
  • Parental responsibility, schooling and health
  • Relocation, recovery and urgent applications
  • Family dispute resolution and section 60I certificates
  • Contravention where orders are not being followed
